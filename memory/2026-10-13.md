## Findings worth keeping
1. Data volume is the lever: 1,749 → 23,403 KiCad sides lifted 86.9 → 89.3 % (L→XL); WACV-only training 61 %.
2. Training converges (val flat from ~80k of 150k steps).
3. Bigger model only helps with a lower lr: XXL lr 3e-4 / 2e-4 / 1e-4 = 86.6 / 88.8 / 89.9 %. XL never tried at lower lr.
4. WACV fine-tuning (alone or 1:1 mixed with KiCad) does not beat KiCad-only on test.
5. Strings (E2) helped on KiCad val but hurt on photos (old data) — untested on data_v1.
6. 3 dev images cannot separate models (XL vs XXL equal on dev, 2.7 apart on test) → use KiCad val (1,369 sides) for selection.
7. Most B8 errors are competition between neighbours; label tables, bracketed pin names and annotation typos follow.
